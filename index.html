Questions

The honest answers.

What if I have an injury or haven't worked out in years? +

This is exactly who the program is built for. Your training coach will adjust everything around your current capacity. We've worked with shoulder reconstructions, bad knees, herniated discs, and decade-long gym hiatuses. The program meets you where you are.

Do I need a gym membership? +

No. The program can run entirely with a basic home setup (dumbbells, a bench, and a band), or scaled up to a fully equipped gym. We adapt to what you have.

How much time per week does this take? +

Three to four 45-minute training sessions per week. Meal prep that fits a normal Sunday. The juice protocol runs five mornings during the Reset phase. That's it. No two-a-days.

Is the juice cleanse mandatory? +

For the 5-day Reset phase at the start of the program, yes. It's the accelerant. We deliver everything you need, and your nutrition coach walks you through it. After Week 1, juice becomes optional but recommended.

What happens after the 8 weeks? +

Graduates can continue with Revyze Continue, our maintenance program at $97/month. Same community, updated workouts, ongoing nutrition support. About half of clients choose to continue. No pressure either way.

Why only ten clients? +

Because the daily check-ins and weekly 1-on-1 calls are real. Two coaches can give that level of attention to ten clients at a time and nothing more. We will not water down the program to grow faster.
