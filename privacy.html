Privacy Policy

Effective: May 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Revyze collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep this in plain language because legal documents shouldn't require a lawyer to understand.

The short version

We collect only what we need to deliver the Program and run the business. We don't sell your data. We don't share your name, photos, or health information without your explicit permission. You can ask us what we have on you and request deletion at any time.

1. Who We Are

Revyze is operated by Revyze LLC ("Revyze," "we," "us," or "our"), based in New Jersey, United States. Contact us anytime at hello@joinrevyze.com.

2. What We Collect

Information you give us directly

When you book a revision call, enroll in the Program, or contact us, you may provide:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your phone number
  • Your shipping address (for juice kit delivery)
  • Payment information, which is processed by Stripe and never stored on our servers
  • Health and fitness information you share in the intake forms (weight, measurements, medical history, dietary preferences, training history)
  • Photos, videos, or testimonials you choose to share with us
  • Messages and check-ins you send during the Program

Information collected automatically

When you visit joinrevyze.com, we automatically collect:

  • Your IP address and approximate location (city/region only)
  • Browser type, device type, and operating system
  • Pages you visit, time spent, and how you arrived at the Site
  • Referring URLs and search terms

This data is collected through Vercel Analytics and (if we run advertising) the Meta Pixel. We use it to understand how visitors use the Site and to improve our marketing.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Deliver the Program (build your meal plan, your training program, coach you, ship your juice kit)
  • Communicate with you about your enrollment, calls, check-ins, and progress
  • Process payments and prevent fraud
  • Respond to your questions and provide support
  • Improve the Program based on aggregated feedback
  • Send marketing emails about Revyze (you can unsubscribe at any time)
  • Run advertising that may reach you on other platforms (you can opt out via your device's ad preferences)
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information to anyone. We share it only in these specific situations:

With service providers who help us run the business

Including: Stripe (payments), Google Workspace (email), Calendly (scheduling), our content delivery platform (Program materials), our email marketing platform, our shipping carrier (for juice kit delivery), and analytics providers. Each of these providers is bound by their own privacy obligations and only receives the information needed to perform their function.

With your consent

If you give us permission to share a testimonial, photo, or success story, we may use it in marketing as described in our Terms of Service.

For legal reasons

If required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Revyze, our clients, or others.

In a business transfer

If Revyze is merged, acquired, or sells assets, your information may transfer as part of that deal. We will notify you if this happens and your data continues to be governed by this policy.

5. Health Information

Sensitive Data

We collect health-related information (weight, measurements, medical history, training history) because we cannot build your program without it. We treat this information as confidential.

We do not share your health information with anyone outside of your assigned Revyze coaches and the team members who directly support your program. We never share it with advertisers, marketing partners, or third parties.

Revyze is not a HIPAA-covered entity, but we treat health data with the same care a medical practice would.

6. Cookies and Tracking

joinrevyze.com uses cookies and similar technologies for:

  • Essential functions: remembering your session and making the Site work
  • Analytics: understanding how visitors use the Site (via Vercel Analytics)
  • Advertising: if we run ads, the Meta Pixel and similar tools may track conversions and let us show you Revyze ads on other platforms

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect Site functionality.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we have about you
  • Request that we correct inaccurate information
  • Request that we delete your information (subject to legal record-keeping requirements)
  • Opt out of marketing emails (use the unsubscribe link or email us)
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we don't sell, but this is your right under California law if we ever did)
  • Withdraw consent for testimonial usage
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@joinrevyze.com. We'll respond within 30 days.

8. Data Security

We use industry-standard security practices to protect your information, including encryption in transit (HTTPS), secure cloud infrastructure, password protection, and access controls limiting who on our team can see what.

No system is 100% secure. If we ever experience a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

9. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as you are an active client and for a reasonable period after, to comply with tax, accounting, and legal record-keeping requirements (typically 7 years for financial records). Health and fitness intake data is retained for as long as needed to support your continued coaching, then archived or deleted upon your request.

10. Children's Privacy

Revyze is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us information, contact us immediately and we will delete it.

11. International Users

Revyze operates from the United States. If you are visiting the Site from outside the US, your information will be transferred to and processed in the US, which may have different data protection laws than your home country.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The most recent version will always be at joinrevyze.com/privacy with the effective date at the top. Material changes will be communicated by email if you are a current client.

13. Contact

Questions, concerns, or requests about your data: hello@joinrevyze.com
